Swat's Up!

A 2D action game about swatting mosquitoes, built solo in Unity — from empty scene to cross-platform build.

Swat's Up! is a 2D action game where players control a pair of hands swatting mosquitoes across weapon pickups, collectible rewards, and challenge maps. Built solo in Unity (C#) with a data-driven catalog system for weapons and maps, mosquito AI powered by tuned state machines, and a single codebase shipping to both Steam (Windows/macOS) and WebGL — including cross-platform save/load and dedicated WebGL performance optimization. Most of the development has focused on tuning feel: weapon pacing, mosquito behavior, and progression that rewards the player without dragging out the loop.

In development — targeting October 2026 launch

  • Unity 6
  • C#
  • Data-driven design
  • State machines
  • Cross-platform builds
  • WebGL optimization
